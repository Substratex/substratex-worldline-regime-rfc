# Provenance

WRS 1.0 is the first unified implementation-neutral RFC series joining Worldline and regime semantics into one current standards-track specification.

Earlier separate Worldline-interpretation and regime-naming documents are historical precursor standards. WRS 1.0 supersedes those fragmented formulations as the current unified specification while preserving their scientific lineage.
