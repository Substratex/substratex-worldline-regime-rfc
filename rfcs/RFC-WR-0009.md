# RFC-WR-0009 — Recovery and Re-Entry

**Status:** Draft  
**Target:** WRS 1.0  
**Namespace:** `urn:substratex:worldline-regime:1.0:`  
**Originator:** Arjay Asadi  
**Publisher:** SubstrateX®


Recovery is a canonical regime.

Recovery confirmation requires declared persistence. Temporary surface coherence is insufficient where persistence is required.

`partial_recovery`, `failed_recovery`, `recovery_pending`, `re_entry`, and `new_stable_formation` are bounded findings/conditions, not new regimes.

Re-entry requires an explicit correspondence criterion and does not imply restoration of hidden memory, identity, or internal state.
