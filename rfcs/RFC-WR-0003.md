# RFC-WR-0003 — Canonical Regime Vocabulary

**Status:** Draft  
**Target:** WRS 1.0  
**Namespace:** `urn:substratex:worldline-regime:1.0:`  
**Originator:** Arjay Asadi  
**Publisher:** SubstrateX®


WRS 1.0 defines exactly five canonical top-level regimes:

- `stable`
- `transitional`
- `phase_locked`
- `collapse`
- `recovery`

Regimes classify observable longitudinal organization, not correctness, safety, alignment, truthfulness, consciousness, or intent.

`brittle`, `open_watch`, `partial_recovery`, `failed_recovery`, `re_entry`, `recovery_pending`, and `boundary_proximal` are secondary conditions/findings, not top-level regimes.

New top-level regimes require a future standards-track extension.
