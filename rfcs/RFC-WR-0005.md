# RFC-WR-0005 — Transitions and Boundary Events

**Status:** Draft  
**Target:** WRS 1.0  
**Namespace:** `urn:substratex:worldline-regime:1.0:`  
**Originator:** Arjay Asadi  
**Publisher:** SubstrateX®


A Regime Transition SHALL identify source regime, target regime or candidate target, coordinate/window, status, evidence support, contract identity, and boundary-event reference where applicable.

Transition status is one of: `candidate`, `confirmed`, `reversed`, `superseded`, `indeterminate`.

A confirmed boundary event does not itself establish observable failure. Reversals and supersession are non-destructive.
