# RFC-WR-0006 — Worldline Continuity and Discontinuity

**Status:** Draft  
**Target:** WRS 1.0  
**Namespace:** `urn:substratex:worldline-regime:1.0:`  
**Originator:** Arjay Asadi  
**Publisher:** SubstrateX®


Worldline continuity is the evidence-supported preservation of a trackable trajectory across a declared horizon.

A discontinuity is a loss of trackable trajectory continuity not sufficiently explained by a declared transition, reset/restart, source gap, coordinate discontinuity, run boundary, or known reconstruction transformation.

Separate runs are not one continuous Worldline by default. Cross-run correspondence does not imply common hidden state or identity.
