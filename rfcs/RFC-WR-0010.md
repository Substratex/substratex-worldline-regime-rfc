# RFC-WR-0010 — Worldline Findings and Claim Boundaries

**Status:** Draft  
**Target:** WRS 1.0  
**Namespace:** `urn:substratex:worldline-regime:1.0:`  
**Originator:** Arjay Asadi  
**Publisher:** SubstrateX®


A Worldline Finding SHALL identify finding type, Worldline reference, evidence references, method/version, temporal scope, status, support semantics, claim boundary, and missingness.

Permitted finding classes include regime timeline, transition window, boundary proximity, continuity, discontinuity, open watch, lead-time, recovery, re-entry, and trajectory shape.

WRS findings MUST NOT, without separate authority, assert hidden reasoning, internal state, consciousness, intent, personality identity, root cause, universal prediction, safety certification, truthfulness, or alignment.
