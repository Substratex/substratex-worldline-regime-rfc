# Governance

The Worldline and Regime Standard (WRS) is originated by **Arjay Asadi** and published through **SubstrateX®**.

Maintainers SHALL preserve semantic stability of canonical terms, distinguish scientific semantics from product implementation, preserve missingness, prohibit hidden-state reinterpretation, publish incompatible changes explicitly, and retain superseded specifications for provenance.

New regimes or marker types require explicit standards-track justification, evidence requirements, claim boundaries, and compatibility analysis.
