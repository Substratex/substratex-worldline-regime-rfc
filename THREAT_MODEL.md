# WRS Threat Model

WRS focuses on semantic and evidentiary failure modes:

- hidden-state inflation;
- snapshot inflation;
- regime renaming drift;
- regime overproduction;
- candidate-to-confirmed promotion;
- retrospective leakage into prospective claims;
- fabricated lead-time;
- missingness collapse;
- run-boundary continuity inflation;
- recovery inflation;
- causal inflation;
- instrument capture.

WRS does not prove source truth, causal mechanism, scientific validity of every detector, safety, alignment, consciousness, or provider-internal state.
